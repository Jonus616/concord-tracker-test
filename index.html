Champion Tiers

Based on cosponsorship count, with separate thresholds by chamber. Real-time tracking, not final grades.

GoldHouse: 6+ bills · Senate: 4+ bills
SilverHouse: 4–5 bills · Senate: 3 bills
BronzeHouse: 1–3 bills · Senate: 1–2 bills

Scoring Criteria

Four categories determine each member’s fiscal responsibility score.
